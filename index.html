Your heart has been sending signs.

Heart disease is the leading cause of death in the U.S. Some of it sends warning signs; some of it, like high blood pressure, only shows up when you check your numbers. We're students putting those signs where everyone can see them: here, and on billboards in our towns as the project grows.

      Get checked for free near you

      High blood pressure and high cholesterol usually have no symptoms. The only way to know is to check. Find a free or low-cost check close to you.

      Enter your ZIP code or use your location to see places on the map.

      Your ZIP code or location is only sent to Google Maps to show nearby places. We don't save it. Hours and services change, so call ahead. A machine reading is a screening, not a diagnosis: if it's high, check again and see a doctor.
