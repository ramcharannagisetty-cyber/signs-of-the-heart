Your heart has been sending signs.

Heart disease is the leading cause of death in the U.S. Some of it sends warning signs; some of it, like high blood pressure, only shows up when you check your numbers. We're students putting those signs where everyone can see them: here for now, and on billboards in our towns once we can fund them.
