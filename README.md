# Signs of the Heart

A student-led cardiology awareness website: plain-language guides to heart failure, FMD and SCAD, high blood pressure, cholesterol, heart attack and CPR, and AFib and stroke, plus free billboard templates.

Live site: https://myheartsigns.com (also https://heartsigns.vercel.app)

Open `index.html` in a browser to view it locally. Billboard designs (preview JPG, full-size 4800x1400 JPG and vector SVG) are in `assets/billboards/`.

This site is for education and is not medical advice. If you think you're having a heart attack or stroke, call 911.
